# Master AI Agent Directive: Ergonomics, Human Playability & Range Safeguards

**Target Audience**: Local AI Agents (Antigravity / Code Generation Agents)  
**Context**: Community Concert Band Arranging (Wisconsin Rapids City Band - Grade 3/4)  
**Evaluation Reference**: SCSBOA Adjudication Criteria (40% Music Performance, Tone, Technique, Dynamics, Breath Control)

---

## 📌 Executive Mandate & The Core Medley Invariant

1. **The Core Medley Mandate (Specific Wind or Brass Lead)**:
   AI agents MUST NOT generate 14-voice full band scores for initial songwriting, testing, or melody sketching. All new compositions MUST be created with the **Core Medley Instrument (CMI)** first (`core_medley.ly` / `core_medley.mid`). A **SPECIFIC WIND OR BRASS INSTRUMENT** from the Wisconsin Rapids City Band is explicitly assigned to carry the medley each time (e.g. `B♭ Trumpet 1`, `B♭ Clarinet 1`, `Alto Saxophone`, `Flute`, `Tenor Trombone`, `Euphonium`). Generic or anonymous "Lead" lines are strictly forbidden; the staff must be explicitly labeled and scored for that designated physical wind or brass instrument.

2. **ABSOLUTE BAN ON PIANO (Wisconsin Rapids City Band Instrumentation)**:
   The Wisconsin Rapids City Band **DOES NOT HAVE A PIANO**. Agents MUST NEVER include piano, `\new PianoStaff`, or Acoustic Grand Piano (GM 0) in scores, Core Medley templates, or MIDI arrangements unless explicitly and specifically requested by the operator. All Core Medleys must use the designated Specific Wind/Brass Lead and Low Brass (Tuba/Bass Trombone) under `\new StaffGroup = "CoreMedley"`.

3. **Physical Ergonomics & Human Limits**:
   When generating, mutating, or arranging LilyPond or MIDI score files, AI agents MUST enforce **strict physical ergonomics and human playability limits**. Computer synthesizers can play infinite 32nd-note runs without breathing, but human wind/brass players require physical breath rests, embouchure recovery, and realistic finger/slide mechanics.

## 🎺 1. Hard Register & Working Range Boundaries (Concert Pitch)

All notes generated by agents MUST remain strictly within the following comfortable Grade 3/4 working ranges:

### Woodwinds
* **Flute**: `E♭4` to `F6` (Avoid continuous fortissimo above `C6`; high register requires high air speed).
* **Oboe**: `D4` to `E♭5` (Avoid rapid low `C4`/`D4` staccato articulations due to reed resistance).
* **B♭ Clarinet**: `D3` to `G5` (Avoid rapid 32nd-note leaps back and forth across the throat-tone break `A4`–`B♭4`).
* **Alto Saxophone**: `C4` to `F5` (Keep lyrical lead lines in the middle register `G4`–`D5`).

### Brass
* **B♭ Trumpet**: `B♭3` to `F5` (High `F5` is a climax note only—never sustain above `D5` for long passages to prevent lip fatigue).
* **French Horn**: `B♭3` to `F5` (Avoid wide interval leaps greater than an octave).
* **Tenor Trombone**: `E♭2` to `G4` (Account for physical slide travel time; avoid rapid 1st-position `B♭2` to 7th-position `E2` jumps).
* **Tuba**: `B♭1` to `F3` (Requires immense air volume; keep low notes rhythmically anchored).

### Strings & Bass
* **Electric / Upright Bass**: `E1` to `G3` (Provide solid walking basslines and syncopated accents).
* **Cello**: `C2` to `E4` (Singing tenor countermelodies and low string support).

### Percussion
* **Timpani**: 4 standard kettles tuned to `F2`, `B♭2`, `C3`, `E♭3` (or piece root/fifths). Do NOT write rapid, instantaneous pedal retuning during fast passages.
* **Marimba**: `C3` to `G5` (Maximum 4-mallet interval reach of an octave per hand).
* **Glockenspiel**: `B♭5` to `D7` (High metallic sparkle over full band tuttis).
* **Concert Battery (Snare / Bass Drum / Toms)**: Allow **2 to 4 measures of rest** when a percussionist must physically switch instruments or swap drumsticks for yarn mallets.

---

## 🫁 2. Human Breathing & Rest Rules

1. **The 16-Bar Breath Rule**: No wind or brass voice may play continuously for more than **16 bars** without at least **2 to 4 full measures of rest** for breathing and embouchure recovery.
2. **High Brass Recovery**: Passages written at `f` or `ff` above the staff MUST be followed by a soft passage (`p`/`mp`) or resting measures.
3. **Multi-Measure Rest Compression**: Always wrap individual instrumental parts in `\compressMMRests` so musicians see consolidated rest blocks rather than empty measure lists.

---

## 🎵 3. Key Signatures, Rhythms & Articulations

1. **Accessible Concert Keys (WR City Band Home Key Invariant)**:
   The Wisconsin Rapids City Band primarily plays in **Concert B♭ Major (`bes \major`)**. All new compositions and arrangements MUST default to **Concert B♭ Major** (or relative **G Minor**, **E♭ Major**, **F Major**). Key signatures with more than 3 sharps or 4 flats are strictly banned.
2. **Rhythmic Clarity**: Avoid irrational or overly complex nested tuplets (e.g., no 7:5 or 11:8 tuplets). Keep syncopations clear and readable.
3. **Dynamic Balance**: Do NOT write low woodwinds at `ppp` in their lowest registers (where tone is naturally heavy) or high brass at `ppp` on high notes (where air support must remain strong).

## 🐍 4. Automated Python Playability Auditor (`validate_playability.py`)

Agents must run this validation script on generated `.ly` or `.mid` files before compiling final PDFs or audio:

```python
import music21

# Grade 3/4 Concert Pitch Limits (midi note numbers)
RANGE_LIMITS = {
    'Flute': (63, 89),        # Eb4 to F6
    'Oboe': (62, 75),         # D4 to Eb5
    'Clarinet': (50, 79),     # D3 to G5
    'AltoSax': (52, 77),      # C4 to F5
    'Trumpet': (58, 77),      # Bb3 to F5
    'Horn': (58, 77),         # Bb3 to F5
    'Trombone': (51, 67),     # Eb2 to G4
    'Tuba': (34, 53),         # Bb1 to F3
}

def audit_score(file_path):
    score = music21.converter.parse(file_path)
    violations = []
    
    for part in score.parts:
        part_name = part.partName or 'Unknown'
        notes = part.flatten().notes
        
        # Check consecutive measures without rests
        consecutive_bars = 0
        for element in part.recurse():
            if isinstance(element, music21.stream.Measure):
                if len(element.notes) > 0:
                    consecutive_bars += 1
                else:
                    consecutive_bars = 0
                if consecutive_bars > 16:
                    violations.append(f"[{part_name}] Exceeded 16 bars without rest at measure {element.number}")
                    
        # Check range limits
        for n in notes:
            if isinstance(n, music21.note.Note):
                pitch_val = n.pitch.midi
                for inst, (low, high) in RANGE_LIMITS.items():
                    if inst.lower() in part_name.lower():
                        if pitch_val < low or pitch_val > high:
                            violations.append(f"[{part_name}] Pitch {n.pitch.nameWithOctave} out of range ({low}-{high}) at measure {n.measureNumber}")
                            
    return violations

if __name__ == '__main__':
    import sys
    test_file = sys.argv[1] if len(sys.argv) > 1 else 'Scores/score_full_band.ly'
    issues = audit_score(test_file)
    if issues:
        print("❌ Playability Violations Detected:")
        for issue in issues:
            print(f"  - {issue}")
    else:
        print("✅ Score Passed All Physical Playability & Ergonomics Checks!")
```
